### Most automation projects die in the handover.

A consultant maps your workflow. A developer builds something that works in a demo. Six months later the model has drifted, an API has changed, nobody owns it, and the team quietly goes back to the spreadsheet.

We started Spaciva in [YEAR] because we kept being called in to fix exactly that — systems that were built correctly and then abandoned. The engineering was rarely the problem. The problem was that nobody had signed up for the part after launch.

So we built the company around that part. Every system we ship comes with a monthly plan to monitor it, tune it, and extend it as your business changes. It's not an upsell at the end of a project — it's how the engagement is scoped from day one.

> **We'd rather run five automations that still work in two years than ship fifty that don't.**

## SECTION 5 — How We Work

> **UI:** This one genuinely is a sequence, so numbered markers earn their place here (unlike the "why choose us" grid, where numbering would be decorative). Vertical timeline on desktop with a connecting rule; stacked cards on mobile. Keep the numerals large and light-weight so they read as structure, not decoration.

**01 — We tell you if you don't need us**
The first call is a feasibility conversation, not a pitch. If a Zapier scenario or a change in process solves your problem faster and cheaper than a custom build, we'll say so. We'd rather lose a $5,000 project than deliver one that shouldn't exist.

**02 — Fixed scope, fixed price**
You get a written scope with a fixed price and a delivery date before any code is written. No open-ended hourly billing, no surprise change orders — if the scope moves, we re-quote in writing first.

**03 — Weekly updates you don't have to ask for**
Every Friday you get a written progress note and a short Loom walkthrough of what moved. You'll never wonder what your offshore team did this week.

**04 — Deployed into your real environment**
We integrate with your existing stack — your CRM, your Shopify store, your Slack, your database — so the output triggers action instead of sitting in a dashboard nobody opens.

**05 — We stay on**
After launch we monitor accuracy and uptime, retrain and retune against drift, and extend the system as your workflows change. This is the part most vendors skip.
